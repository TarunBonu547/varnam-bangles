THE HEART OF VARNAM

Jewellery with a sense of occasion.

Varnam Bangles is an Indian bangle and jewellery brand for the colour, ritual and quiet joy woven into everyday life.

A point of view

Beautiful things can hold a memory.

At Varnam, we look to the details that make Indian adornment feel personal: a glint of gold at the wrist, a bright stack chosen for a celebration, the familiar rhythm of bangles as a day begins.

Our edit brings together expressive colour and considered forms, made to move easily between cherished traditions and the way you dress now.

INSPIRED BY INDIA

Tradition, seen with fresh eyes.

Indian jewellery has always known how to speak through colour, texture and symbolism. We appreciate that language and make room for a modern rhythm: pieces that feel at home at a wedding, a festival, a family gathering or an ordinary day worth marking.
